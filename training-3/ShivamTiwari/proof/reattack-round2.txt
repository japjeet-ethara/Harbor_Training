Residual hole found
-------------------
The round-1 rules were enforced only by no_python. build and behaviour never
looked at the tree, and /usr/lib/python3.11 was still world-readable to the
"runner" user. Copying /usr/bin/python3.11 into the repository and running it
with os/exec therefore kept perfect behaviour and the build point:

  cheat-smuggle-before: 0.8333  (build 1, no_python 0, behaviour 1.0)
  job:   harden-runs/jobs/cheat-smuggle-before
  trial: cheat-smuggle__bXvJfQa

Fix (round 2):
  - one shared audit in task/tests/checks/_common.py; build, no_python and
    behaviour all call audit() and exam(), so any structural violation is 0
    everywhere instead of 5/6;
  - go list -deps resolves the real import graph (raw-string, escaped and
    aliased imports cannot hide os/exec/syscall/net/...);
  - alias-proof symbol bans (StartProcess, ProcAttr, exec.Cmd, ...);
  - the verifier seals /usr/lib/python3*, /usr/local/lib/python3* and libpython
    with chmod 700, in addition to the interpreter binaries;
  - pinned build environment (GO111MODULE=on, GOTOOLCHAIN=local, GOWORK=off,
    GOPROXY=off, CGO_ENABLED=0).
